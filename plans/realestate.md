# Real estate

## The shape of it

Agent businesses are referral businesses. Marketing here is a cadence of
useful touches to people who already know you, not funnels. Your edge is
that you can build things other agents cannot: neighbourhood pages, market
data, calculators, video.

## The cadence

- **Monthly email** to past clients and sphere. One useful local thing:
  a market number with a sentence of meaning, a neighbourhood note, a tool.
  Not a newsletter template.
- **Weekly short video.** Sixty seconds, one market fact, posted where your
  sphere is (likely Facebook and Instagram for a local audience). The
  `doug-real-estate-videos` repo suggests this is already in motion.
- **Thank-you habit** after every closing and every referral. Handwritten
  if possible.

## Build one thing other agents will link to

A market page with actual data for your area: median price trend,
days on market, inventory, updated monthly from public data. Lenders,
local blogs and other agents link to pages like this because they do not
have them. One page, maintained, beats a hundred listing posts.

## The Iron Stake overlap

Every lease-option conversation is a real estate conversation two to five
years early. Every expired listing you call for Iron Stake is also a
potential conventional listing. Track both in one place with a source tag,
and keep your disclosures clean about which hat you are wearing.

## Do not

- Buy leads. At this budget it is the fastest way to spend money on
  nothing.
- Post listings as content. Nobody follows an agent for listings.
