# Web design and custom builds

## What exists

Baseline Designs (repos `baselinedesigns`, `baselinedesigns-outreach`,
`sales-baselinedesigns`) and the CodeMode "custom builds from $5,000"
offer. A portfolio of sixteen shipped sites, one app and several client
redesigns. This is the business that pays this quarter, so it gets the most
reliable channel, not the cleverest one.

## The one channel: personalized video audits

Nothing beats this for a solo operator with no budget.

1. Build a list of 20 businesses a week. Criteria: a visibly weak site, a
   business that clearly makes money (so they can pay), and a niche you can
   speak to. Real estate brokerages, property managers, title companies,
   investors and home services are the obvious start because you already
   speak their language and have the network.
2. Record a two to three minute Loom per business: their site on screen,
   three specific things that cost them leads, one of your sites as proof.
   No slides, no pitch deck.
3. Send it by email with a one-line subject: "Three things on
   [company].com". Follow up once after five days.
4. Expect one to three replies per 20. Offer a paid audit or a fixed-scope
   first project, never a free consultation.

Thursday is audit day in the weekly rhythm. Twenty a week is four hours.

## Productize one offer

"$5,000+, four to six weeks" is honest but vague. Add one fixed offer under
it that is easy to say yes to, for example a landing page or a brokerage
site in ten days at a fixed price. It is the entry ticket; the custom work
comes after.

## Proof

- One case study on codemodeapps.com with a number in it. If no client
  numbers are shareable, use your own: "sixteen sites, X monthly visitors,
  built and maintained by one person."
- Ask the last three clients for a two-sentence testimonial this week.

## Referral loop

Every real estate contact, every Iron Stake seller and every lender you
meet runs a business with a website. Mention it once, never twice.
